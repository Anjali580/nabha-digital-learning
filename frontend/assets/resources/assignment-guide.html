Assignment submission guide

A simple checklist for sending your best work on NabhaLearn.

Before you start

  • Read every instruction on the assignment page.
  • Check the due date and the maximum marks.
  • Plan your work on paper first, then write or type the final version.

What you can submit

Written responseType your answer in the text box, up to 2000 characters.
One filePDF, JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 5 MB.

You can submit both a written response and a file. Choose the format your teacher asked for.

Make your work clear

  1. Start with a title, your name and your class.
  2. Use headings or numbered points so each part is easy to find.
  3. Label every diagram. Draw with a pencil and take a straight, well-lit photo.
  4. Explain ideas in your own words instead of copying from a book.
  5. Check spelling, units and calculations before submitting.

Taking a good photo

  • Place the page on a flat surface near a window.
  • Hold the phone steady and fill the frame with the page.
  • Avoid shadows from your hand or the phone.
  • Make sure all writing is sharp when you zoom in.

After you submit

  • You will see the submission status change to Submitted.
  • You can replace your file while the status is Submitted or Late.
  • Once your teacher reviews it, the submission is locked and feedback appears.
  • A late submission is still accepted and marked as late.
Keep a copy. Save your final file on your phone or computer until you receive your marks and feedback.
